# Data governance and privacy

## Principle

FireViewer retains the smallest durable record needed to explain and reproduce
a decision. Public availability is not permission to republish content, and
analysis is not an excuse to create a shadow archive of news pages, videos or
transcripts.

## Durable records

Subject to source rights and project policy, FireViewer may retain:

- source and evidence tickets with canonical URLs, publishers, timestamps,
  hashes, rights and processing outcomes;
- versioned evidence, geographic hypotheses, assessments, review decisions and
  audit events;
- failure records needed to distinguish unavailable, incomplete and malformed
  provider responses;
- official products or derived bands when their terms permit retention;
- reviewed spatial-package manifests and compact reproducibility receipts;
- authorised user media and derived crops or keyframes under an explicit
  retention rule.

## Content not retained by default

The acquisition pipeline is not intended to durably retain:

- complete scraped pages or news articles;
- copied public images or video binaries without a separate right to retain
  them;
- complete media transcripts;
- arbitrary third-party page assets;
- model conversation history containing raw third-party content.

## User media and permissions

Permission to analyse, retain, show during review and republish are separate.
An upload must record the contributor's authorisation, time and geographic
information when supplied, declared accuracy and any relevant camera metadata.

Private or sensitive information remains access-controlled. Public derivatives
require a separate rights and editorial review.

## Provenance and integrity

Durable evidence should identify, as applicable:

- source and canonical URL;
- observation, publication, acquisition and retrieval times;
- content or response digest;
- rights and attribution;
- processing provider and immutable revision;
- transformations applied;
- coordinate reference system and uncertainty;
- predecessor and successor revisions.

Corrections create new referenced revisions rather than silently replacing old
records.

## Public presentation

Incident pages distinguish official observations, user contributions,
reconstructions, simulations and machine-derived assessments. A citation does
not imply that the source endorses FireViewer's interpretation.

## Third-party rights

Every article, model, dataset, map, satellite product and asset keeps its own
licence and terms. FireViewer code and documentation licences do not relicense
upstream material.

## Security, privacy and removal

Do not publish credentials, private evidence, personal machine paths, precise
sensitive locations, private contributor information or internal provider
identifiers.

For a rights concern, provenance correction, data-removal request or private
security report:
