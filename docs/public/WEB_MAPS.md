# Web atlas: dated public verification

**Last documented verification: 8 September 2026.**

This page records the public atlas state that was checked on 6–8 September 2026. It is a dated verification record, not proof that the same deployment, provider availability or browser behaviour remains unchanged today.

Public atlas: **https://fireviewer.vercel.app/incendies**

## Verified public coverage

The documented release connected eleven French territories to their FireViewer dossiers. By the 8 September verification, all eleven had a published terrain view, incident geometry and a checked chronology archive.

The verified territories were:

- Die / massif de Justin;
- Boussès;
- Bénonces / Serrières-de-Briord;
- Trévillach;
- massif de Fontainebleau;
- Biscarrosse;
- massif du Claps / Bellegarde-en-Diois;
- Luglon;
- Saumos;
- Gros Bessillon / Pontevès;
- Cagnano / Cap Corse.

The atlas distinguishes measured terrain, dated incident geometry, documentary reconstructions, sourced activity and positioned photographs. A published layer does not become an observation merely because it is visible in the viewer.

## 2D and 3D verification

The 8 September review recorded desktop and mobile checks of the eleven public maps. The 2D view used continuous IGN Plan imagery for the visible extent while the 3D views used the published measured terrain packages.

A continuous basemap provides geographic context; it does not establish continuous observation of the fire.

## Die / Justin documentary sequence

The documented Die / Justin release contains 59 dated documentary reconstructions from 24 June to 21 August 2026, plus separate source products and sourced chronology entries.

The complete calendar does **not** establish a complete measured outer perimeter for every day. Some states are retrospectively constrained documentary reconstructions. Activity is not automatically carried forward to later dates.

This public documentary sequence is separate from the uncalibrated Part.4 pipeline described in [Daily reconstruction](RECONSTRUCTION.md). It does not qualify unattended publication.

## Natural affected-area presentation

The 8 September release documented a natural-mode presentation in which cumulative affected geometry can be shown as dark burned ground with canopy removed inside the published footprint. Altitude mode retains its own styling.

This rendering is a display convention. It does not create a new perimeter, connect separate geographic components or infer burned area from animation.

## Activity rendering

Flame animation is a symbolic representation of sourced activity. It can use documented flame points, supported fronts or anchors inside active polygons.

Thermal detections alone do not create flames, and a previously active location is not automatically displayed as active on a later date. The animation is presentation only and must not be interpreted as a physical fire simulation or prediction.

## Daily perimeter products

The documented atlas uses dated incident products independently of measured terrain production. Terrain packages and wildfire-state layers remain separate.

A day can reuse the last known affected contour when no newer geometry is available, while retaining the original source date. Activity is not carried forward merely because the affected contour remains visible.

The 8 September verification recorded 240 dated products across the eleven cases, including 59 for Die / Justin. Of those dates, 205 resolved to geometry and 35 initial dates were explicitly unavailable. These counts describe documentary products, not 240 independently measured fire perimeters.

## Located photographs

Ground photographs remain separate evidence objects. Markers use only supported or explicitly approximate geographic placement. Photographs without a defensible location remain in the dossier rather than receiving invented coordinates.

Screenshot publication does not relicense third-party photographs or source material.

## Cumulative natural footprints

The 8 September review recorded cumulative natural-footprint products for all eleven cases. These combine existing historical burned polygons available up to the selected product date while preserving separate geographic sectors.

They do not use a convex hull, spatial buffer, expansion from activity points or future observations. Source geometry, source dates, product identities and reported areas remain distinct from the presentation layer.

## Provenance and rights

Map attribution remains visible in the public presentation. IGN, Copernicus, EFFIS, CEMS, NASA and other upstream material retain their own terms, attribution and source semantics.

The selected screenshots are documentation artifacts. Their inclusion does not transfer rights in the underlying geographic or third-party source material.

## Interpretation limits

This dated atlas verification establishes that the documented pages and selected interactions were observed working during the stated review. It does **not** establish:

- present-day hosting availability;
- complete observation of every incident day;
- independent validation of every documentary reconstruction;
- Part.4 calibration or qualification;
- physical-mobile coverage across all devices;
- scientific or operational accuracy;
- emergency-service suitability.

For current project maturity, use [Implementation status](STATUS.md). For the distinction between measured geography and incident reconstruction, see [Architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md) and [Map Builder boundary](MAP_BUILDER.md).
